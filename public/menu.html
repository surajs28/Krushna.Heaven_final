The story of gelato

Gelato is Italian for “frozen”.

Traditionally from northern Italy, gelato is churned more slowly than ice cream, with less air and less fat, so the creaminess and the flavour come through first.

  • Slow-churnedLess air means a denser scoop and a deeper flavour.
  • 100% vegetarianEvery gelato on our menu is vegetarian.
  • SorbettoOur sorbettos are made from water, sugar and fresh fruit.
  • VariegatoThe ripples and toppings folded through a gelato have a name: variegato.
