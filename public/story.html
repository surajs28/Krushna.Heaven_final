Our story

Five cravings. One home.

Krushna’s Heaven is built on a simple idea: everyone at the table should get what they love, and still share the table. Gelato, woodfire pizza, brews, waffles and savoury plates live together in Wanowrie, Pune.

Begin the story
01

Chapter one · The idea

One table, every craving.

Some visits begin with a scoop. Others begin with pizza from the oven, a coffee between friends or fries for the middle of the table.

At Krushna’s Heaven nobody has to choose a different café to get what they love. Five cravings share one address and one welcome, made for families, friends and evenings that run a little late.

02

Chapter two · The gelato

Slow-churned, scooped to order.

The gelato counter is where many visits start: 45 flavours in four families, from chocolate classics and fresh fruit to creamy favourites and premium picks.

Our premium gelato features Mr Gelato, our gelato franchise partner, and its promise of the taste of Italy. Have it in a cone, a sundae, a shake or a falooda.

03

Chapter three · The kitchen

From the oven to the table.

The woodfire side of the menu brings sourdough pizzas, calzones and artisanal panuozzo.

Pasta, croissant sandwiches, burgers, nachos and fries complete the savoury chapter: plates made for passing around.

04

Chapter four · Pour & linger

Pour, sip, stay a while.

Hot and cold coffee, iced teas, mocktails, fresh juices and protein smoothies keep the conversation going.

Then come the sweet plates: waffles, mini pancakes, the sizzling brownie with ice-cream, blueberry panna cotta and the Gourd-Gée tiramisu.

05

Chapter five · Together

Made for evenings together.

As the lights come up, the café takes on its evening glow: gold-edged tables, blue and grey chairs and always room for one more scoop.

For bigger occasions, ask us about the banquet hall.
