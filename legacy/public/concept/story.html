A café with room for every craving.

Krushna’s Heaven brings five distinct food experiences under one warmly lit roof in Wanwadi.

The place is part of the menu.

Great café visits rarely follow a plan. Someone orders coffee. Someone wants pizza. A child spots the gelato counter. Dessert arrives for the whole table.

That freedom is the idea behind Krushna’s Heaven: a place where different cravings belong together, from morning brews to late-night scoops.

Mr Gelato lives here.

The premium gelato selection at Krushna’s Heaven is offered through the Mr Gelato franchise. Its role is clear: a focused gelato experience within the wider café.
